Why DoNotNotify?

Privacy First Design

Your notifications contain sensitive OTPs and personal messages. That's why DoNotNotify processes everything offline. We have no servers, no analytics, and no tracking. Your data never leaves your device.

Granular Control

Create powerful rules based on app names, message content, or advanced regex patterns. Whitelist what's urgent, blacklist what's noise.

Zero Noise

Stop the constant buzzing from promotional notifications and marketing spam. Keep only the notifications that actually matter to you.

Commitment to Privacy

We believe privacy is a fundamental right. DoNotNotify does not collect or share any personal information. We don't know who you are, and we don't want to.
